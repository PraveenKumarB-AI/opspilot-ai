# Exporting reports

## Export formats
Any report can be exported to Excel (.xlsx), CSV, or PDF from the Export button at the top right of the report screen. Exports include the filters that were applied when you clicked Export.

## Large exports
Reports with more than 100,000 rows are exported in the background. You receive an in-app notification with a download link when the file is ready. Links expire after 7 days.

## Scheduled exports
Report owners can schedule a daily, weekly, or monthly export under Reports > Schedules. Scheduled files are delivered to the email addresses listed on the schedule.
