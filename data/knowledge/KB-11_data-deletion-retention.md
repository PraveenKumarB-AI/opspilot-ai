# Data deletion and retention requests

## Retention
Customer data is retained for the length of the subscription and for 90 days after it ends, unless the contract states otherwise.

## Deletion requests
Requests to delete customer data must come from an Administrator in writing and are handled by the Privacy team, not by support. Support agents must not delete data or confirm deletion timelines.

## Exporting data before deletion
Administrators can export all asset data from Settings > Data Export before requesting deletion.
