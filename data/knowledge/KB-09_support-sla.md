# Support severity levels and response times

## Severity levels
Severity 1: the system is down or a close cannot be completed, with no workaround. Severity 2: a major feature is not working and a workaround exists. Severity 3: a question or minor issue.

## Response targets
Severity 1 tickets receive a first response within 1 hour, 24 hours a day. Severity 2 within 4 business hours. Severity 3 within 1 business day.

## Escalation
Customers can ask to escalate a ticket by replying "escalate" on the ticket. Escalated tickets are reviewed by a support lead within 2 business hours.
