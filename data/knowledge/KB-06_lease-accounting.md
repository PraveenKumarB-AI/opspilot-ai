# Lease accounting

## Adding a lease
Go to Leases > New Lease and enter the commencement date, term, payment schedule, and discount rate. LedgerGrid calculates the lease liability and right-of-use asset automatically.

## Modifying a lease
Use Leases > Modify for changes in term or payments. A modification creates a new schedule from the modification date and keeps the original schedule for history.

## Lease reports
The Lease Rollforward report shows opening balance, additions, payments, interest, and closing balance by period.
