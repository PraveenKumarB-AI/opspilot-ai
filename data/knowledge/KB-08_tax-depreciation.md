# Tax depreciation books

## Tax books
Each asset can carry separate book and tax depreciation. Tax books are set up under Tax > Books, with a depreciation method and convention per book.

## Differences between book and tax
The Book-Tax Difference report lists each asset's book and tax depreciation and the difference for the period. Use it to support deferred tax calculations.

## Filing questions
LedgerGrid calculates tax depreciation but does not file tax returns. Questions about how to report amounts on a return should go to your tax advisor; our support team cannot give tax filing advice.
