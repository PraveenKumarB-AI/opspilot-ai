# User access and login

## Password reset
Users who sign in with a LedgerGrid password can reset it from the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

## Single sign-on
If your company uses single sign-on (SSO), passwords are managed by your identity provider, not LedgerGrid. Contact your internal IT team to reset an SSO password or unlock an account.

## Adding users and roles
Only users with the Administrator role can add users or change roles, under Settings > Users. Role changes take effect at the user's next sign-in.
