# Billing, refunds, and service credits

## Invoices
Invoices are issued annually in advance and are available to billing contacts under Settings > Billing.

## Refunds and credits
Support agents cannot approve refunds or service credits. All refund and credit requests are reviewed by the Billing team, who respond within 5 business days. Service credits for downtime are defined in each customer's contract.

## Changing billing contacts
An Administrator can update billing contacts under Settings > Billing > Contacts.
