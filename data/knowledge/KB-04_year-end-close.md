# Year-end close checklist

## Before closing the year
Complete all depreciation runs for the final period, post pending retirements and transfers, and reconcile asset balances to the general ledger using the Asset Reconciliation report.

## Closing the year
Go to Periods > Year End and select Close Year. Closing locks all periods in the fiscal year. Only an Administrator can close or reopen a year.

## Reopening a closed year
An Administrator can reopen the most recent closed year from Periods > Year End. Reopening is recorded in the audit history and should be approved by your controller.
